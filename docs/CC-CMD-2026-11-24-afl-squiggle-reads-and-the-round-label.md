## Item 7 — whether the `cf` cache options take effect on Squiggle (unmeasured)

Every relay Squiggle read sends `cf: { cacheTtl, cacheEverything, cacheKey }` (Rule 78's pattern), and the finals read (`src/afl-squiggle.js`) copies it. The review (finding F1) found that `api.squiggle.com.au` is another Cloudflare customer's zone (A records 104.21.4.226 and 172.67.132.143, NS pam/chip.ns.cloudflare.com, read 2026-11-24 through `dns.google`) and that Cloudflare's docs say such a zone "fully controls how its own content is cached within Cloudflare; you cannot override it", and that `cacheKey` is Enterprise-only. Squiggle states "the regular API has a cache in front of it" (its own 2026-03-29 post). So the hourly relay-side cache the earlier commit message and comments described is **unmeasured and probably not in effect**; the claims are removed (comments reworded in `src/afl-squiggle.js`; the finals commit message corrected). Volume is user-driven: a finals day is two Squiggle reads per `/v2/games?sport=afl` request, and the handler's own cache is `max-age=15`. Also unmeasured, and fail-closed (a missing row is null and warned): when a final's Aggregate row first exists in Squiggle once its teams are known (Squiggle pre-creates finals games with null teams and no tips, read 2026-11-25), and what an hour of caching would do to a row that appears after the first read; and whether ESPN's and Squiggle's kickoff instants agree for a RESCHEDULED game (21 of 21 played games agree exactly).
**Ruling needed (owner):** UNMEASURED, and not staged: nothing in the relay can read `cf-cache-status` (no deployed route returns it), so no verifier can say PASS, and a staged claim without an executor is an orphan (`scripts/staged-verifier-check.mjs`). Measuring it is a new dispatch-only diagnostic (a Worker-side read of `https://api.squiggle.com.au/?q=tips;year=2026;team=1;source=8` made twice with `cf.cacheEverything`, the second `cf-cache-status` logged and read with `wrangler tail`): `HIT` means the options take effect and stay; anything else means they do nothing on this host, and the options should be removed and the "cache and re-use data" requirement met by caching in the relay's own KV as Kali's reads do (`relayFetchKV`). Not built here because it adds a route (Rule 39) and AFL has no fixtures until 2027.
