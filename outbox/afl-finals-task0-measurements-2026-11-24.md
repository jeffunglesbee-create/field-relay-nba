## 8. What the build rests on, and what it does not

- **Established (measured, re-read):** a final's EVENT carries `season.type` 3 / `season.slug` `post-season` (11 of 11 in 2026, 9 of 9 in 2025) while the league-level season in the same bodies stays type 2; ESPN's week for a final (1-5) is not Squiggle's round (25-29 in 2026, 25-28 in 2025); ESPN's kickoff equals Squiggle's `unixtime` exactly (18 of 18 games); Squiggle's `date` is Australia/Melbourne wall-clock; `?q=tips;year=Y;team=T;source=8` returns the club's whole season of Aggregate tips with `round` and `gameid` in one request of about 9 KB; Kali is offline.

The staged verifier reads the first final in the window in the 2027 finals and says so. (2) When Squiggle publishes a finals game's tip before the game: only completed games were read; the build serves `null` and warns when no row is found, never a tip through another round. (4) Whether 2027's postseason numbering matches 2025's and 2026's (Squiggle's docs say the season starts at round 1 from 2027): the build does not use an offset.
